Read <the business's mail> for people who wrote to the business and wait on an answer: new leads, customers with a question or a problem, and deals under way. Keep each one in this routine's memory with who they are, what they want and when they last wrote, so a thread already told comes back only when it moved.

Report each lead or customer waiting on them, the longest waiting first, and each deal that has gone <a set number of days> with no word either way. File one action item for each reply they owe, with what the person asked and a short reply drafted for them.

Their reply in the thread, or their word, closes one. Never send a reply, a quote or an invoice.
