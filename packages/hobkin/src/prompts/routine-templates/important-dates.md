---
title: Important dates
summary: Birthdays, anniversaries and events of the people who matter to them, two weeks ahead, with time to plan a present or a booking.
recurrence: Daily, early in the morning
goals: life
needs: calendar, contacts
effort: medium
fits: Someone who wants to be a week or two ahead of the dates that matter, like a partner's birthday, instead of remembering the day before.
tells: A date of someone they named, 14 days away, and again at 7 days unless it is handled.
gives: report, action
closes: Their word that it is handled, or the date passing.
private: yes
asks: Whose dates should it keep, and which ones: birthdays, anniversaries, or events such as a recital or a trip?
version: 1
---

Read <their calendar> and <their contacts> for the birthdays, anniversaries and events of <the people they named>, and only theirs.

When one is 14 days away, say whose it is, the date, and what it may need, such as a present to order, a card to send or a table to book. If <their notes> say what that person likes, add one idea. In the same run, ask in Action Items whether it is handled.

When it is 7 days away, remind them once more, unless they said it is handled.

If one of these people has no date in the calendar or contacts, ask for it in Action Items once, and keep the answer in this routine's memory.
