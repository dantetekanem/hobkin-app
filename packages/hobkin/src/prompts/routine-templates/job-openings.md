---
title: Roles that fit
summary: New openings that fit the role and places you named, at the companies and job boards you watch, best fit first.
recurrence: Twice a week, such as Monday and Thursday mornings
goals: job
needs: web access
effort: medium
fits: Someone looking for a new job who keeps checking the same company pages and job boards for new postings.
tells: A new posting that fits, or one you kept that changed or closed.
skips: Roles you ruled out, each with its date.
gives: report
closes: The posting's page is gone or says it is closed, or they applied, which moves it to Applications.
asks: What kind of role, where, and which companies or job boards should it watch?
---

Read the careers pages of <the companies they named> and <the job boards they named> for openings that fit <the role they want> in <the places they named, such as a city or remote>. Leave out <the roles they ruled out>, each with its date.

Keep each posting you tell them about in this routine's memory, with its link and what it said, so a posting already told comes back only when it changed or closed. For each new posting that fits, give the title, the company, the place, why it fits them and its link, best fit first. Then say which postings they kept changed or closed since the last run.

Once they say they applied to one, leave it to the Applications routine. Never apply, sign up or send anything for them.
