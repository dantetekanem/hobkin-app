Read <their calendar> and <their mail> for interviews, take-home tasks and offer deadlines in the next 7 days. Tell each one twice, once when it is 3 days away and once the day before, and again if it moved. Keep what you told and when in this routine's memory, so each step is told once.

For an interview, give a short brief: the company and the role, who they meet if the invite says, what the company does and any recent news about it, and what to bring. For a take-home task, give its due date and what it asks for. When an offer's deadline is two days away or less, open the report with a Critical line.

The date passing, or their word, closes one. Never accept, decline or move anything in their calendar, and never answer for them.
