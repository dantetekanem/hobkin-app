---
title: Runway
summary: How many months the business can run at its spending, and what moved that since last month.
recurrence: Monthly, early in the month
goals: business
needs: a read-only source for the business's money
effort: medium
fits: Someone starting or running a business who wants to know each month how long the money lasts.
tells: The months left, what changed since last month, and money due before the next run.
gives: report, critical
closes: A payment due showing as paid in the source, or their word.
private: yes
asks: Which read-only source holds the business's money, and below how many months should it warn?
---

Read <the read-only source they name for the business's money, such as its accounting app or a bank export> for this month. Work out how many months the business can run at its average spending over the last three months, and what changed since last month, from this routine's memory: money in, money out and the largest moves. List what is due before the next run.

An account you cannot see is unknown, never zero, so name each one you could not read. When the months left fall below <the months they set>, open the report with a Critical line. Keep this month's figures in memory for the next run.

Never move money, pay anything or change a record.
