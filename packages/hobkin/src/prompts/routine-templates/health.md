Read this week's data from <the read-only source they name, such as a sleep or fitness tracker>. Report neutral trends against the weeks before, and name each day with nothing recorded as a gap. Suggest at most one small, low-risk change, and give no diagnosis. Put no more of their data in the report than the trend needs.
