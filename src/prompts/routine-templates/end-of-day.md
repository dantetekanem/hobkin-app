recurrence: Weekday evenings, about an hour before they stop
group: day
effort: medium
fits: Someone who wants to stop work knowing nothing from today is still waiting on them.

Read today's reports with `hobkin reports`. For each one with a `Next:` call, check that call against its live state, such as whether the reply was sent, the review was done or the build now passes. Then say either that nothing from today still needs them, or what is still open.
