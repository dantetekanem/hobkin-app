Read the week's real work in <their sessions, such as `~/.pi/agent/sessions`>. Pick at most one candidate for a post, a case study or a paper, and write a short brief, never a draft: the concrete case, what it found and what still needs proof. When nothing clears the bar, stay quiet and say `Nothing new.`
