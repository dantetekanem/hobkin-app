hobkin runs your checks on a schedule and saves what they find in Reports. For anything else, just ask here.

- `/setup` goes through what hobkin needs and your first checks, and `hobkin setup` lists what is missing
- `/clear` hides this chat
- `/reset` starts a fresh conversation
- `/work` turns work mode on or off
- `/learning` learns from how work turns out, and `hobkin learning` shows what it learned
- `/feedback` and what happened shows the exact report for hobkin's developers, and sends it only on your yes
- `⌘U` attaches an image, and `esc` stops hobkin while it works
- `brief me` catches you up on what came in while you were away, and you answer it in your own words
- `⌘1–5` open the screens, `r` or `m` in a report and `r` in a routine talk it over here, and `x` in the agents list stops an agent
- In Reports, `/` filters by title, and `↑` from the first report reaches All, Scheduled and Work Done
